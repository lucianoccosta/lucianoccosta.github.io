I am an Associate Professor at the Department of Production Engineering of the Federal University of Paraíba (UFPB), in João Pessoa, Brazil, and Adjunct Head of the Logistics and Optimization Group (LOG).
I am also Adjunct Coordinator of the Graduate Program in Production Engineering and Systems at UFPB.

I received my B.Sc. (2013) and M.Sc. (2015) in Production Engineering from UFPB, and my Ph.D. in Applied Mathematics (2020) from Polytechnique Montréal, Canada, under the supervision of Guy Desaulniers and Claudio Contardo.
Before joining UFPB, I was a postdoctoral fellow at HEC Montréal and an Assistant Professor at the Federal University of Pernambuco (UFPE).

Research interests
------

**Methodology:** combinatorial optimization, exact methods, branch-and-cut, column generation, metaheuristics, robust optimization, multi-objective optimization, and decision choice models.

**Applications:** vehicle routing and its variants, machine and personnel scheduling, lot sizing, layout and location problems, humanitarian logistics, sustainable logistics and scheduling, optimization in the management of academic resources, and resource optimization in health care services.
